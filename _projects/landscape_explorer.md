## Landscape Explorer

An interactive mapping tool that lets users explore past and present landscapes from the Great Plains to the Pacific Coast, comparing historic and current aerial imagery spanning roughly seven decades since the mid-20th century.

The tool documents three primary types of landscape transformation:
- **Woody encroachment** — one of the biggest threats to grass and shrublands
- **Cropland expansion** — reducing and fragmenting wildlife habitat
- **Urbanization** — consuming native habitat with effects that are nearly impossible to reverse

I contributed the **data engineering** for this project — processing, organizing, and serving the geospatial data that powers the interactive comparison views.

**Live site:** [landscapeexplorer.org](https://www.landscapeexplorer.org/)

**Organization:** [Working Lands for Wildlife](https://www.workinglandsforwildlife.org/)

**Tech stack:** Geospatial data pipelines, remote sensing imagery processing
