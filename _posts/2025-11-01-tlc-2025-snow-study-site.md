This fall I joined the crew for the 2025 Tender, Loving, Care (TLC) trip to the newly renamed Jeff Dozier Snow Study Site. This is my first field session with Leidos, and the trip was a good introduction. The site has run continuously for 39 years, which makes it one of the longest-running full energy balance snow measurement stations in the world.

We repainted the site and stenciled it with its new name.

Most of my work happens on a screen, and a screen teaches you to expect control. Every part of a pipeline was built by someone, so every part can, in principle, be fixed. That is why a job that fails for no visible reason is so infuriating. It feels like a broken promise. A mountainside makes no such promise. In the field, most of what happens is outside my control, and it ought to be. Accepting that takes a humility I rarely feel at a keyboard, and it was good to feel it again.
