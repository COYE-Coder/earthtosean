Welcome to **Earth to Sean**! This is where I keep my thoughts on science, music, birds, life, and my attempts at Vietnamese in one place.

## Why this site?

My professional career and interests are hard to define, but I find that everyone I enjoy working with has a multifaceted and diverse life. If I'm ever looking for new positions, I'd like to be able to show people some more sides of me than what they see during virtual interviews.

I used to spend a lot of energy trying to convince people that my devotion, discipline, and intuition that I built over years of practicing piano was a garden from which any skills can grow. Now, looking backwards, this is definitely true.

My first paying jobs out of college were working maintenance at the [Southwestern Research Station](https://www.amnh.org/research/southwestern-research-station) and washing dishes at the [Portal Peak Cafe](https://www.portallodge.com/). In Portal, I met countless birding fanatics and learned directly from them. That experience was my springboard into field ecology (mostly ornithology), which I eventually worked into a Master's degree in Systems Ecology. Underneath all of that is my training in classical piano performance. So this site is my attempt to organize the disparate parts of my life, putting the science and the personal side by side to see what happens.

## What to expect

I'll mostly write a bunch of half-baked ideas about music, birding, language, identity, and work. Don't expect many tutorials. I have much to learn in all of these areas, and most of these posts will be me writing down random thoughts.
